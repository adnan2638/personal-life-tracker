# Personal Productivity & Academic Tracker

A personal productivity and academic tracking application designed to help
me build consistent habits, improve my academic performance, maintain a
healthy routine, and stay accountable to my goals.

## 🎯 Why I Built This

I wanted a simple system to track the areas that have the biggest impact
on my daily life:

- Academic performance
- Study consistency
- Physical fitness
- Sleep
- Phone usage
- Personal development
- Spiritual routine

Instead of relying only on motivation, the app turns these goals into
measurable daily and weekly actions.

## ✨ Features

### 📚 Academic Tracker
- Daily study targets
- Syllabus completion tracking
- Recall and revision tracking
- Math practice tracking
- Quiz and viva preparation
- Exam tracking

### 💪 Fitness & Health Habits
- Exercise tracking
- Sleep tracking
- Daily routine tracking

### 📵 Phone Control
- Daily phone-use limit
- Fixed phone-use window
- Track whether the daily limit was maintained

### 🕌 Spiritual Routine
- Daily Islamic habit tracking
- Personal accountability

### 🎁 Reward System
- Daily rewards for completing targets
- Weekly rewards based on consistency
- Customizable food-based rewards

### 📊 Progress & Analytics
- Daily completion percentage
- Weekly progress
- Habit streaks
- Performance trends
